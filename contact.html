Good to know

Questions, answered.

What does a typical project look like?

We start with a short conversation to understand your brand and goals, then share a plan, creative direction and timeline before any production begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A set of social creatives moves faster than a brand identity or a film. After the first conversation we give you a realistic timeline.

Do you work with brands outside Lucknow?

Yes. We work with brands across India and collaborate remotely. On-location shoots happen in and around Lucknow.

Can you work with our existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. We are happy to build on what you already have, or refresh it if it is time for a change.

How do we get started?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, email us, or fill in the form above. Tell us a little about your brand and what you have in mind and we will take it from there.
